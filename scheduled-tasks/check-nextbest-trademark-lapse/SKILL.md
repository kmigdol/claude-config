Remind Kayleigh to check, and do the check yourself: has the USPTO trademark NEXTBEST (US Serial 88755665, Reg. No. 6107572, owner NextBest AI Inc., Class 35 "provision of an online marketplace for buyers and sellers of goods and services", registered Jul 21, 2020) lapsed?

Background: its Section 8 (6-year) declaration of use was due Jul 21, 2026. As of 2026-10-13 nothing had been filed, and the grace period (with late fee) ended Jan 21, 2027.

Steps:
1. Open https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=88755665&caseType=SERIAL_NO&searchType=statusSearch in the built-in browser and wait a few seconds for the status to load. (Or fetch https://tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn88755665 from inside that page.)
2. Read the TM5 status, the Status line, and the Prosecution History. Look for any Section 8 declaration filed between Jul 2025 and Jan 21, 2027.
3. Open the MAINTENANCE tab to see if the §8 is still listed as due.

Report to Kayleigh in short, plain language, answer first:
- If a §8 was filed: the mark is being kept alive. Give the filing date.
- If no §8 was filed and the status says DEAD/Cancelled: the registration has lapsed.
- If no §8 was filed and the status still says LIVE: this is expected. The USPTO usually takes a few months to post a cancellation. Suggest checking again in about 2–3 months.
Add one line saying this isn't legal advice and the owner may still have common-law rights if they keep using the name.
